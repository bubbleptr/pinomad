---
title: A new model picker, six-box codes, and emails that are easier to read
version: v0.17.0
date: 2026-11-14
---

**Model picker** (web, Mac app and iPhone)

- Agent, model and thinking level are picked in one place. On the web and Mac app the composer's capsule opens Agent, Model and Thinking rows, with models listed by provider. On iPhone the model button opens a panel above the composer with agent chips, the models and thinking-level chips.
- What a named agent pins is shown but locked (**Set by @name**). An agent whose model the host doesn't offer is listed but can't be picked.
- The thinking level you picked is kept across model switches: a model without it runs at the nearest level below, and switching back restores it.
- Models that two providers serve under the same name show the provider under the name.

**Account**

- Emails from the server (sign-up, password reset and confirmation codes, and security notices) are now formatted: the code is set large and can be copied in one piece, and the email follows your light or dark setting. The wording and subjects are unchanged.
- On the web and Mac app, mailed codes are entered in six boxes. Paste and one-time-code autofill still work.
- A host that is removed from the account and joins again is announced as a new host again, on the web, Mac app and iPhone.
- Signing in as another account on the same server no longer forgets the first account's paired hosts.

**Tasks and the side panel** (web and Mac app)

- The side panel has a close button, and terminal tabs are cards. With only one kind of task, Tasks drops its group headings.
- Tasks lists processes that have ended, and the Overview no longer shows the processes document as raw data.
- A new chat hides Tasks, Changes and Terminal until it has a conversation.

**iPhone**

- The composer is a row of three pieces: Add, a round model button and the draft field with Send inside it.
- The Overview lists subagents and processes under one **Tasks** section.
