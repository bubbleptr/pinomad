---
title: iOS shows your account only while you are signed in
version: v0.9.1
date: 2026-10-31
---

**iOS**

- After signing out, or when the server ends your session (for example after the account password changes), the home avatar and Settings no longer show your username. They show the plain avatar and **Sign in**, matching the push warning, and the sign-in form still remembers your server.
