---
title: Use the official server
description: Sign in to app.pinomad.si, join your host to your account, connect the Mac app and iPhone, and turn on push notifications.
---

The official server at `https://app.pinomad.si` lets your devices find and reach your host from anywhere, without running a server of your own. It vouches for your devices and forwards ciphertext when they can't reach the host directly; conversations stay end-to-end encrypted between your devices and your host.

You need an account on the server. Sign-up is by invite: ask for an invite code, then create your account with it.

## 1. Create an account or sign in

To create an account, open [app.pinomad.si](https://app.pinomad.si/) (or the sign-in form in the Mac app) and click **Create an account**. Enter the invite code, a username and a password:

- The username uses lowercase letters, digits, dots, dashes or underscores, 2–32 characters.
- The password needs at least 12 characters.
- An invite code works once and expires after 7 days. If it's used or expired, ask for a new one.

You're signed in as soon as the account exists. Next time, sign in:

- **Web:** open [app.pinomad.si](https://app.pinomad.si/) and sign in with your username and password.
- **Mac app:** before any host is connected, the home screen shows the sign-in form; otherwise click **Sign in** at the bottom of the sidebar. The **Server** field already says `https://app.pinomad.si`; type only your username and password.

The Server field is editable. If you run your own server, put its address there instead (see [Remote access](/zh/docs/remote-access/), Chinese).

## 2. Join your host

The host needs Node 25+ on macOS or Linux. After signing in, click **Add a host**. You get a one-time command; run it on the machine that should run the agent:

```sh
npm i -g pinomad && pinomad join https://app.pinomad.si <token>
```

The command works once and expires after 10 minutes; click **New command** if it runs out. `pinomad join` registers the host with your account, remembers the server, and installs the host as a user service so it starts at boot. Add `--name "Mac mini"` to set the name your devices show. Running it again on the same machine is safe.

Click **Done** and the host appears in your list.

## 3. Connect

Next to the host, click **Pair**. The server asks the host for a one-time invite and the client pairs with it, so no QR code is needed. A host you've paired before shows **Connect** instead.

Then sign in to a model provider under Settings → Providers, as in [Getting started](/docs/).

## 4. iPhone

In the iPhone app, tap **Sign in** in Settings. The server is already `https://app.pinomad.si`; type the same username and password. The app can't create accounts yet, so sign up on the web or in the Mac app first. Once signed in, the hosts on your account are listed; tap **Pair** on each one you want on the phone. The iPhone app is in internal TestFlight for now.

## Push notifications

The iPhone app can notify you when a conversation is waiting for your answer, finishes, or fails. Push needs:

- the host joined to your account with `pinomad join`;
- the iPhone signed in to the **same account** as the host;
- push turned on for that host in the app (it's off by default and set per host).

A notification carries only the conversation title and the event, never the question or the reply. The title passes through the server and Apple. More details: [Usage](/zh/docs/usage/) (Chinese).

## Password

To change your password, click **Change password** in the account panel on the web or in the Mac app, or tap **Change Password** on the iPhone's account page. Enter your current password and a new one (at least 12 characters). This device stays signed in, and your other devices are signed out. Sign in on them again with the new password, and on the iPhone that also turns push back on.

If you forgot your password, there's no reset email, because the server doesn't store an email address. Ask the server's admin (whoever sent your invite) to reset it. They give you a new password; sign in with it, then change it to one only you know.

## Devices on your account

The account panel on the web and in the Mac app, and the iPhone's account page, list every browser and app that has signed in to your account: its name, when it last signed in, and the hosts it joined through the account, with its state on each (registered, waiting for the host to revoke it, or revoked). The one you're using is marked **This device** (**This iPhone** on the phone).

If you lose a device, click or tap **Remove** next to it and enter your password. The server signs it out of your account and asks each host it joined through the account to revoke it. A host that's online does it right away; one that's offline does it the next time it connects. The host still has the final say: you can pair the device with it again later with a QR code. To remove the device you're using, sign out instead.

The server only knows what went through the account:

- A device paired with a QR code, or paired through the account before your host and the server supported this list, isn't on it. Revoke it in the host's Settings → Devices.
- A host running an older version doesn't report the devices it admits, so the server can't ask it to revoke one. The Remove form names those hosts; revoke the device there, or update the host with `pinomad upgrade` so later pairings are covered.
- Apps from before this list show up as **Unknown device**, once per sign-in.

## Delete your account

To delete your account, click **Delete account** at the bottom of the account panel on the web or in the Mac app, or tap **Delete Account** at the bottom of the iPhone's account page, and enter your password. The server deletes the account along with its sessions, host bindings and push registrations, and every device is signed out. The admin account can't be deleted.

Hosts joined to the account lose their connection to the server right away. To reach one through a server again, run `pinomad join` on it with another account. Your devices stay registered on each host, because the host keeps that list itself: a device that reaches the host directly (same network or Tailscale) still connects. To remove a device, revoke it in the host's Settings → Devices, or remove it under **Devices** before you delete the account.
